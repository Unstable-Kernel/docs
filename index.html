Architecture

Experiments

01

Flocking

Reynolds boids: separation, alignment, cohesion. 1000+ agents self-organize into coherent flocks from purely local rules.

02

Pheromone Paths

Ant-colony optimization. Agents deposit chemical trails that diffuse and evaporate, forming efficient transport networks.

03

Ecosystem

Predator-prey dynamics with energy, reproduction, and bidirectional mutation. Populations oscillate naturally.

04

Mycelium Network

Adaptive graph where signals strengthen active edges and prune idle ones. Self-organizing communication infrastructure.
